UK ETA & Electronic Travel Authorisation overview

The UK ETA (Electronic Travel Authorisation) is a digital entry permission linked directly to your biometric passport for short stays across England, Scotland, Wales, and Northern Ireland.

Max Single Stay
Up to 6 Months
Total Validity
2 Years (or Passport Expiry)
Entry Permission
Multiple Entries
Clearance Format
100% Digital Chip Link

Digital Clearance for Short-Stay Visits

Visa-exempt nationals travelling to the United Kingdom for tourism, family visits, business meetings, or short-term study must hold an approved electronic travel authorisation before boarding.

  • Covers England, Scotland, Wales, and Northern Ireland
  • Permits stays of up to 180 days per visit
  • Digitally linked to your passport chip—no paper stamp required

Simple Biometric Document Preparation

Travelers only need a valid biometric passport from an eligible country, a clear digital facial photograph, an active email address, and a valid payment card.

  • Machine-readable passport bio-page with clear MRZ lines
  • Recent plain-background digital portrait photo
  • No physical embassy visit or courier delivery needed

Consult our complete Check UK ETA documents before scanning your passport bio-page and digital portrait.

Two-Year Multi-Entry Flexibility

Once granted, your UK ETA remains valid for two full years or until your passport expires, allowing unlimited repeat journeys without re-submitting before every trip.

  • Enter via UK international airports, Eurostar, or seaports
  • Compatible with ePassport automated border gates
  • Renew only when replacing your passport or after 2 years

Fast Digital Verification Before Departure

Carriers verify your UK ETA status electronically when you check in at the airport or train terminal. Preparing early keeps your itinerary smooth and uninterrupted.

  • Electronic verification tied to your passport number
  • Email confirmation delivered directly upon approval
  • Supports tourism, business, transit, and permitted creative work
