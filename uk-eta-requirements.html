UK ETA Requirements: Passport, Photo & Document Checklist

Before starting your online UK eTA submission, prepare a valid biometric passport from an eligible country, a compliant digital portrait photo, an active email address, and a payment method.

Biometric Passport Bio-Page Specifications

Your passport must be issued by a UK eTA-eligible nation, feature an electronic biometric chip symbol on the cover, and remain valid for the duration of your stay in the UK.

  • Clear colour scan or photo of the full passport bio-data page
  • Both lines of the Machine-Readable Zone (MRZ) must be sharp and legible
  • No glare, fingers, shadows, or cropped corners on the passport scan

Review the complete documents required for UK ETA to ensure your passport bio-page scan meets official standards.

Digital Applicant Photograph Rules

Every applicant must upload a clear, recent passport-style digital colour photo taken against a plain light-coloured background with a neutral facial expression.

  • Head and upper shoulders centered and facing directly forward
  • No sunglasses, tinted lenses, hats, or non-religious head coverings
  • Even lighting without heavy facial shadows or digital filters

Once your photos are ready, see how to apply for UK ETA and upload your files through the online portal.

Basic Conditions to Meet Before Applying

Applicants must intend to visit the UK for up to 6 months for tourism, family visits, business, short study, or transit, and must not plan to take paid UK employment.

  • Travel must fall within permitted visitor or transit categories
  • Disclosure of any criminal convictions or past immigration breaches
  • Must travel to the UK using the exact passport used in the application

Verify whether your nationality is on the list of countries eligible for UK ETA before preparing your documents.

What Happens After Documents Are Uploaded?

Uploaded passport scans and facial photographs undergo quality verification to prevent common rejection triggers such as blurred MRZ characters or improper cropping.

  • Automatic format and resolution adjustment for uploaded images
  • Cross-check of passport number and expiry date against MRZ code
  • Prompt email notification if a clearer scan is needed
